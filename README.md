# 📊 Blinkit Sales & Outlet Performance Analysis

> **An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Blinkit's sales performance, outlet characteristics, product categories, and customer ratings to identify business trends and optimization opportunities.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Blinkit's grocery sales data using **Microsoft Power BI** to understand overall sales performance, product-level trends, outlet performance, customer ratings, and the relationship between outlet characteristics and sales.

The dashboard was designed around a business requirement to analyze **sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory/product distribution** through interactive KPIs and visualizations.

The analysis focuses on identifying patterns across:

* Product / Item Type
* Fat Content
* Outlet Location
* Outlet Size
* Outlet Type
* Outlet Establishment Year
* Customer Ratings

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to evaluate Blinkit's sales performance and identify factors influencing revenue across different products and outlet characteristics.

The dashboard answers questions such as:

* What is the overall sales performance?
* Which item categories generate the highest sales?
* How does fat content affect sales?
* Which outlet types contribute the most revenue?
* How does outlet size relate to sales?
* Which outlet locations perform better?
* How has sales performance changed based on outlet establishment year?
* How do average sales, item count, and customer ratings vary across outlet types?

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales by Fat Content

Analyzes total sales based on product fat content and compares the performance of:

* Low Fat
* Regular

The analysis also allows comparison of supporting KPIs such as average sales, number of items, and average rating.

### 2. Sales by Item Type

The dashboard compares sales across different product categories, including:

* Fruits & Vegetables
* Snack Foods
* Household
* Frozen Foods
* Dairy
* Canned
* Baking Goods
* Health & Hygiene
* Meat
* Soft Drinks
* Breads
* Hard Drinks
* Others
* Starchy Foods
* Breakfast
* Seafood

This helps identify which product categories contribute most to overall revenue.

### 3. Fat Content by Outlet

Sales are further segmented by outlet and fat content to understand how product composition performs across different outlet tiers.

### 4. Sales by Outlet Establishment Year

A time-based analysis evaluates how outlet establishment year relates to total sales and highlights changes in sales performance across different establishment periods.

### 5. Sales by Outlet Size

The dashboard analyzes sales contribution across:

* Small
* Medium
* High

This helps evaluate whether outlet size is associated with stronger sales performance.

### 6. Sales by Outlet Location

Sales are analyzed across outlet location tiers:

* Tier 1
* Tier 2
* Tier 3

This provides insight into the geographic distribution of sales.

### 7. Outlet Type Performance

A detailed comparison of outlet types includes:

* Total Sales
* Number of Items
* Average Sales
* Average Rating
* Visibility

This enables a consolidated comparison of outlet performance.

---

## 🔎 Key Dashboard Findings

Based on the dashboard:

* Total sales stand at approximately **$1.20M**.
* **Supermarket Type1** contributes the largest share of sales among the displayed outlet types.
* **Tier 3 outlets** generate the highest sales among the three location tiers shown.
* **Medium-sized outlets** account for the largest share of sales among the outlet-size categories displayed.
* **Fruits & Vegetables** and **Snack Foods** are among the highest-performing item categories.
* The overall average customer rating is approximately **3.9**.
* The dashboard shows noticeable variation in sales across outlet establishment years, with a visible peak around **2018**.
